Since 1887

A backdrop with a past as rich as the occasion.

Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House, a celebrated mineral-springs health resort, and later listed on the National Register of Historic Places, The Crescent House is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings. For more than a century it has been a place where the community gathered to celebrate. Your event becomes the newest chapter in a story the whole city knows.

Hosts choose The Chimes because it offers something a rented venue never can: genuine history, one-of-a-kind architecture, and a sense of occasion the moment guests walk through the doors.
