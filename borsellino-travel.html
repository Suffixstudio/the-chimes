Since 1887

A building built for people on their way somewhere.

The Crescent Bath House was built in 1887 as a mineral-springs resort, a destination people traveled across the state to reach. More than a century later, the building is still in the business of sending people somewhere worth going.

Come sit down, look at a map, and talk it through in person. It is a better way to plan a trip than a browser tab.
