It was one of Southern California's celebrated mineral-springs health resorts. Built in 1887 over a natural hot spring, the bath house drew visitors from across the country who came to soak in its 132-degree sulfur waters and mineral baths, long considered a remedy for arthritis, rheumatism, and other ailments. That same landmark building is now The Chimes.

It is. Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House, The Chimes is listed on the National Register of Historic Places and is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings.

It has a reputation. Decades of visitors have shared ghost stories tied to the old bathhouse, and it's long been considered one of the most storied buildings in the valley. We'll let you decide.
