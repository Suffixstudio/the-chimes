Since 1887

An address people already know.

Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House and later listed on the National Register of Historic Places, The Chimes is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings. Locals do not need directions to it. They grew up walking past it.

For a business trying to look established in this valley, that is not a small thing.
