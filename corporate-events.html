Since 1887

A first impression your clients will remember.

Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House, a celebrated mineral-springs health resort, and later listed on the National Register of Historic Places, The Chimes is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings. For more than a century it has been the room where this community gathered. Your meeting joins a story the whole city knows.

Companies choose The Chimes because a hotel conference room says nothing about you. A restored landmark says quite a lot.
