Since 1887

A venue with a past as rich as your future.

Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House, a celebrated mineral-springs health resort, and later listed on the National Register of Historic Places, The Crescent House is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings. For more than a century it has been a place where the community gathered to celebrate. Your wedding becomes the newest chapter in a story the whole city knows.

Couples choose The Chimes because it offers something a brand-new venue never can: genuine history, one-of-a-kind architecture, and a sense of occasion the moment guests walk through the doors.

Open flame is not permitted anywhere on the property except the Rose Garden, where candles are welcome; sparklers are not allowed anywhere, including the Rose Garden. Flower petals are fine outdoors, but rice and confetti are not permitted. Anything you hang has to come down without leaving a mark, so nothing may be nailed, drilled, or glued to the building.
