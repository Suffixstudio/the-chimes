Fountain Plaza

Since 1887

A venue with a past as rich as your future.

Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House, a celebrated mineral-springs health resort, and later listed on the National Register of Historic Places, The Chimes is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings. For more than a century it has been a place where the community gathered to celebrate. Your wedding becomes the newest chapter in a story the whole city knows.

Couples choose The Chimes because it offers something a brand-new venue never can: genuine history, one-of-a-kind architecture, and a sense of occasion the moment guests walk through the doors.

Ceremonies are usually held in the Rose Garden, cocktail hour in The Library Speakeasy, and dinner and dancing in the Fountain Plaza under the open sky, with indoor and covered options so the day works rain or shine. The Chimes VIP Room upstairs is where the wedding party gets ready. Every package includes five hours of event time, plus an hour beforehand to set up and an hour afterwards to clear.

The Rose Garden holds up to 300 guests, Fountain Plaza up to 150 standing and 100 seated, and The Library up to 30 standing and 10 seated. The Parlor Room holds up to 30 standing and does not do seated dining, only seating on the furniture in the room. Share your guest count when you inquire and we will confirm the right space and setup.

Yes. The Chimes VIP Room upstairs is reserved for the wedding party on the morning of, with antique furnishings, a chandelier, and stained glass, so the getting-ready photographs are worth keeping. One thing worth raising early: the second floor is not ADA compliant, so if anyone in your party has accessibility needs, tell us when you inquire and we will talk through what the building can and cannot accommodate before you book.

Catering runs through our preferred vendor list, which we share when you inquire, so you are choosing from people who already know the building and how it works. For the bar, you may bring your own licensed bar vendor. Every bar vendor must present a valid ABC permit, liability insurance, and certified bartenders in advance, and cocktail service happens in The Library Speakeasy. Because the building is historic, some outside rentals may need to bring their own power source, so tell us what you are planning to bring and we will flag anything that needs arranging.

Yes. A private reserved lot sits directly across the street from the venue, so your guests park just steps from the entrance rather than circling the block in formalwear.
