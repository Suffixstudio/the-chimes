Since 1887

A venue with a past as rich as your future.

Built in 1887 as the Crescent Bath House, a celebrated mineral-springs health resort, and later listed on the National Register of Historic Places, The Chimes is one of Lake Elsinore's original buildings. For more than a century it has been a place where the community gathered to celebrate. Your wedding becomes the newest chapter in a story the whole city knows.

Couples choose The Chimes because it offers something a brand-new venue never can: genuine history, one-of-a-kind architecture, and a sense of occasion the moment guests walk through the doors.

The Bridal Suite

Get ready upstairs, then stay the night.

The Victorian suite upstairs, with its antique furnishings, chandelier, and stained glass, is where the wedding party gets ready on the morning of. And when the last dance ends, the same suite can be rented as your honeymoon suite, so the celebration ends just steps from where it began.
