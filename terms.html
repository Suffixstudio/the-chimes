Venue and rental policies

These are the standing policies for events, rentals, and use of the property, and they are a summary. The rental agreement you sign for your date is what governs, and it may add terms specific to your event.

  • Insurance. Event insurance is required for events and rentals. The coverage required, and who must be named on the policy, are set out in your rental agreement and confirmed with you before your date.
  • Music and noise. Music and other loud noise end at 10 PM.
  • Smoking. Smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor areas. It is not permitted anywhere inside the building.
  • Animals. Service animals are welcome throughout the property. Pets and emotional support animals are not permitted.
  • Security. Security requirements for your event are set out in your rental agreement.
  • Guest counts. Guest counts are confirmed for your layout and your date as part of booking.
  • Open flame. Open flame is not permitted anywhere on the property, indoors or out, with one exception: candles are welcome in the Rose Garden. Sparklers are not permitted anywhere, including the Rose Garden.
  • Rice, confetti, and petals. Flower petals are welcome outdoors. Rice and confetti are not permitted anywhere on the property.
  • Damage. The deposit that holds your date is applied to the price of your package. It is not a damage deposit, and nothing is held back against damage. Damage to the building, its fixtures, or the grounds beyond ordinary wear from normal use is charged to the renter separately, at the cost of repair or replacement, and may be claimed against the renter's event insurance. The details are set out in your rental agreement.
  • Décor and fixings. Anything you bring must come down without leaving a mark. You may hang décor using the fixings already in place and other non-damaging methods. Nothing may be nailed, screwed, pinned, drilled, or glued to walls, woodwork, tile, or any part of the structure, and no new holes may be made anywhere in the building.
  • Setup and clear-down. Access for setup begins one hour before your event. Breakdown and clear-down happen within your booked event hours, not after them. Everything brought in leaves the same night; nothing may be stored on the property overnight.
  • Children. Children are welcome and must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • Photography by the venue. We photograph the building, and occasionally events in it, for our own use. Tell us in writing before your date if you would prefer that we do not, and we will not.
